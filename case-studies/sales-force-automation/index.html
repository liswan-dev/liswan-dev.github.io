Sales Force Automation

Sales Operational Platform

01 — Problem

Field activity with no real-time visibility

Field sales activity — store visits, stock handovers, and payment collection — was tracked manually through paper forms and phone calls, so management had no real-time visibility into what reps were doing or how stock and cash moved between the warehouse and the field.

02 — Solution

A structured, synced field workflow

Sales Force Automation gives every rep a structured flow for visits, stock handover, and daily closing, while management gets consolidated reporting and store management tools — all synced through Google Apps Script for lightweight, no-install deployment.

03 — Features

06 — Outcome

The impact so far

Sales visits and stock movements are now recorded as they happen instead of reconstructed at day's end, giving management a much clearer, near real-time picture of field activity and reducing the back-and-forth needed to reconcile daily closing and payments.
