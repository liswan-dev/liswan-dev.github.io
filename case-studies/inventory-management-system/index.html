Inventory Management System

Inventory Management Platform

01 — Problem

Inventory disconnected across spreadsheets

Inventory across purchasing, material receiving, and project allocation was tracked in disconnected spreadsheets, making it difficult to know real stock levels, catch buffer-stock shortfalls early, or trace which supplier or purchase order a batch of material came from.

02 — Solution

One system for the whole inventory flow

The Inventory Management System brings purchase orders, material receiving, project allocation, and supplier records into one system, with buffer-stock thresholds that flag low inventory before it becomes a shortage.

03 — Features

What the system does

01

Inventory Tracking

Real-time stock levels across all materials.

02

Purchase Order

Create and track purchase orders from request to fulfillment.

03

Material Receiving

Log incoming goods against open purchase orders.

04

Project Allocation

Assign material usage to specific projects.

05

Buffer Stock

Automatic flags when stock nears minimum thresholds.

06

Supplier Management

Maintain supplier records and order history.

The impact so far
